Getting started

  1. Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open the app and set up your TV. On a Sony Bravia you switch on IP control on the TV, set a key (PSK) and enter it in the app. On Android TV and Google TV the TV shows a code the first time you connect, and you enter it in the app (pairing).
  3. In the settings under “Appearance”, choose your design with its colour scheme and control style: gestures with a large swipe area, or compact with all keys, plus arrow keys or a touchpad.
  4. A tour in the settings shows you the most important features again.

TV is not found or a key does not respond

  • The TV must be on or in network standby, and phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Some routers separate devices from each other (for example guest Wi-Fi or “client isolation”). Then the phone cannot see the TV.
  • An active VPN on the phone can interfere with the home network. Turn it off to test.
  • On a Sony Bravia, IP control must be switched on at the TV and the PSK entered correctly in the app. The error log names this, for example as “IP control off on Sony” or “wrong PSK”.
  • On Android TV and Google TV it often helps to pair the TV again if pairing was rejected or failed. Some TVs only allow one phone at a time as a remote.
  • From Android 17 the app needs the “Nearby devices” permission to reach TVs on your home network. If it is missing, the app shows code G305 and a button to allow it.
  • If the TV cannot be switched back on after turning it off, enable “Remote start” (Sony) or “Network standby” / “Wake on Wi-Fi” on the TV.
  • Open the error log in the settings. It shows error codes with an explanation and a hint for solving them. “Clear” deletes it again.

Several TVs, restart and inputs

  • Tap the name at the top left to switch between your saved TVs or add a new one.
  • In the “Compact” view you must tap “Restart” twice for safety. Android TV has no restart command of its own, so the app holds on/off for five seconds. Depending on the manufacturer, a menu with “Restart” appears, or the TV only switches off. A Bravia that is also set up as a Sony is the most reliable.
  • Under “Inputs” you see your TV’s inputs. Tapping one switches straight to it. Android TV has no common command for this: if switching does not work on your device, the app opens the TV’s input menu, where you pick the input with the arrows. With a Sony Bravia set up as Sony, switching always works directly.

Touch lock

The touch lock prevents accidental input. To unlock, swipe up on the screen.

Widgets and notification panel

  • There are three widgets: Mini (on/off, volume, mute), Remote (with channels, back, home and input) and D-pad.
  • Add widgets as with any Android app: long-press an empty spot on the home screen, choose “Widgets” and look for the Universal Remote.
  • The remote in the notification panel is optional and can be switched on in the settings.

Voice commands, macros and streaming search

  • Start voice commands by tapping the microphone. You can add your own phrases in the settings.
  • Macros run several steps with one tap.
  • Streaming search sends a title you type straight to the streaming app.
