Frequently asked questions

Do the apps cost anything?
No. All apps are free and contain no ads.
Do you collect my data?
What exactly is stored is explained in the privacy policies of the app and the website (both in German).
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
Write to me via the contact page.
Which devices do the apps run on?
On Android phones and tablets running Android 8 or later. The apps are released on Google Play. For the Universal Remote you also need a TV on the same Wi-Fi: a Sony Bravia or a device with Android TV or Google TV.
Where can I find help for the app?
The app help covers connecting to your TV, widgets, touch lock and the error log.
